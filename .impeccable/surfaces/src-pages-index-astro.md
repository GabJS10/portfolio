# Surface brief: portfolio home (src/pages/index.astro)

Scope: the whole single-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience (the shipped work leads).
Audience: recruiters and freelance clients, equally. Action: open the CV or write an email after seeing real projects.
Constraints: dark base (light background unwanted), keep personal photo, keep copy and project data. User feedback on round 1 (freight-container world, rejected): no container concept, no stencil type, different colors, far less ornament; wants minimalist. Code-led build (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a dimly lit exhibition room: each project is a work hung under its own light with a small precise wall label; the interface recedes. Refuses the dev-portfolio card grid and every decorative chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Matte near-black walls (#0d0d0e), off-white label ink, warm gray secondary, one warm lamp tone used only for what is active (work in light, link hover, Disponible). One grotesk family (Hanken Grotesk), light weight at display scale, regular for labels, tabular numerals for years. Hairlines only; no cards, no radius, no icons beyond arrows.

STORY: Visitor meets Gabriel in an almost empty entrance (name, one line, CV, email, small portrait with its label), walks the room work by work (screenshot lit as it comes into view, label with title, year, technique = stack, description, link), reads the intro wall (about, stack), leaves through contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, name in light grotesk at ~7rem over two lines, role line, tagline, CV + email actions, warm dot Disponible. Right, small portrait (~220px) hung under a faint lamp pool with a museum label. Generous empty wall; the first work's top edge hints below the fold.

FORM: Exhibition room / museum wall labels, position 3 of the re-rolled ordered list; seed key 2d2e4396 (reroll 1). Raises: one light only for the active (raku), fixed shared grid for every room (PC-98), measured year rule in the margin (daylight section).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
